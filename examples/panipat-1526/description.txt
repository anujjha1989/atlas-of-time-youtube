How did Babur, a prince from Kabul with about 12,000 people, defeat Ibrahim Lodi, Sultan of Delhi, with an army said to be 100,000 strong and 1,000 war elephants? This documentary tells the full story of the First Battle of Panipat (April 1526), the battle that ended the Delhi Sultanate and founded the Mughal Empire, using Babur's own memoir, the Baburnama.

You'll see the cart-and-gun wall Babur borrowed from the Ottomans, the steppe flanking sweep called the tulughma, the failed night raid, and the battle hour by hour, recreated in 3D with maps built from real terrain.

NOTES ON THE NUMBERS
The 100,000 men and 1,000 elephants are what Babur says people estimated; his 12,000 counts everyone who crossed the Indus with him, including servants and traders. Beveridge's translation dates the battle to Friday 20 April 1526; it is often given as 21 April.
